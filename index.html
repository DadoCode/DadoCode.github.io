Process modelling · MATLAB

Tyre pyrolysis in a rotary kiln, heated from the inside

1270kJ/kg · down from 1400

Wall-heated kilns lose energy through the shell. Would induction-heated susceptors inside the bed do better?

Built both kiln models in MATLAB and compared them on conversion, temperature profile and energy demand.

Lower energy, but 88.5% conversion against 100% for the benchmark. Overall the new design was worse, and that is what I reported.

Energy consulting · 20+ sites

Energy conservation measures, costed before anyone spends

3.3year payback

Clients knew their bills were high. Nobody knew which fix paid for itself first, or whether any of them did at all.

Baselines built from utility bills and logger data, each measure sized against it, priced through a bill of quantities, then appraised on money and carbon together.

184,000 kWh a year identified at one site, 579,000 at another, ranked so the client knew what to do first.

Materials research · Barcelona

Why the first seven runs produced nothing

24experiments

Synthesising biodegradable packaging film from cellulose. Seven attempts in, still no film.

Ran FTIR on the product. It matched plain cellulose, which meant the reaction was not happening at all.

Catalyst loading in the published method was too low. Raised it, and every run afterwards produced film.
